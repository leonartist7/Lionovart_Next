## Shared brief

LIONOVART — Reference-led visual direction
Updated 6 October 2026. Working design system derived from four images explicitly supplied by the owner as style references. Use the actual images as the visual authority; exact CSS tokens and font matches below remain implementation proposals until compared in a rendered specimen.

REFERENCE SET / MagicPath project 457814053256044544
01 Transition: image 458189568840204288. Warm ivory content beneath a flowing black/gold/silver edge with a fine red streak; bold black two-line headline and small gray italic serif subline.
02 Information: image 458189617984839680. Compact left-aligned sans-serif heading, selective red words, readable small body copy, generous separation; a vivid red comparison column beside a quiet cream column.
03 Hero: image 458189668001935360. Near-black stage, very large heavy white uppercase display type, one expressive gold italic serif word, lion emblem, metallic ribbons framing a clear center, vivid red rounded primary action.
04 Services: image 458189728030806016. Warm ivory, large rounded portfolio image, heavy black uppercase heading, centered gray body copy, small outlined pills and low-contrast edge filaments.

CORE DIRECTION
Minimalism means few competing elements and obvious reading order. Preserve strong typography and the metallic visual signature. The references combine two intentional section treatments within one identity: a dramatic dark hero and calm, spacious cream content. Neither a dark-only site nor a uniformly plain cream site represents the full reference set.

TYPOGRAPHY
Heavy, broad, tightly set sans-serif display headings provide the main identity. Use short stacked lines with deliberate line breaks; uppercase suits hero, service and major chapter statements. Image 02 establishes sentence-case bold headings for compact explanatory content. Do not force every heading into uppercase or hero scale.
A high-contrast italic serif supplies brief expressive accents and supporting phrases; reserve it for a few words or a short subline.
Body and utility text use a quiet readable sans-serif, sentence case and comfortable line height. Labels can be small uppercase with light tracking.
OWNER-CONFIRMED TYPOGRAPHY (6 October 2026): Clash Display Bold (700) for major headings; Clash Display 400/500 for descriptions, body text, navigation and controls. Playfair Display Italic (400) is the secondary expressive face. Do not substitute DM Sans for body text. These are confirmed font choices.
Starting ranges to test: hero 56–144px responsive / line-height .9–1; major section 36–72px / .95–1.05; compact explanation 24–40px; body 16–20px / 1.45–1.65; utility labels 11–13px. These are proposed ranges, not measurements from the raster references. Keep 44px control hit areas.

COLOR AND MATERIAL
Warm ivory and near-black are the two main surfaces. Vivid red is the brand signal for primary action, selected words and an occasional purposeful information panel; avoid replacing it with burgundy by default.
Gold is warm metallic champagne with dark bronze and pale highlights. Silver/ivory highlights can share the ribbons. Do not use saturated yellow as a blanket substitute for metallic gold.
Existing ivory #F5F0EB, red #E5192A, black #0D0D0D and white #FFFFFF are practical starting tokens to test against the images. Provisional light-content text #111111 and secondary #66615B. Gold should be a material/range; a possible midtone #D6AE68 is a proposal, not an extracted authoritative color.
The prior Work palette #F7F4EF / #24221F / #AD1826 describes the current local implementation and is not the brand-wide source of truth.

COMPOSITION AND DECORATION
Keep the reading area clear. Metallic ribbons frame the outer edges of hero scenes or form a section transition, as in 01 and 03. Cream sections use fine faint gold filaments, as in 04. Do not place full-strength ribbons behind paragraphs, filter labels or every work card.
Use large quiet fields, deliberate asymmetry in editorial/explanatory sections and centered compositions where the references establish them. Do not center every page automatically.
Use the lion as a deliberate hero symbol only when an appropriate approved asset is available; do not repeat it as a mascot or replace the actual brand mark with an image-generated variation.
Warm paper, foil, subtle material shading and soft image depth are reference qualities. Avoid filling every surface with glass, glow or heavy shadow.

COMPONENTS
Large portfolio/service media can have softly rounded corners, as in 04. Starting radius 20–28px is a draft range; compact labels need much less rounding. One universal radius is inappropriate.
The hero primary action is a red capsule with readable white text and a restrained pale edge; any depth is subtle. Utility/service pills are light and fine-outlined in the cream treatment, with a clear active state. Their contrast should remain subordinate to the work.
Cards do not require extra caption containers, thick borders or dashboard-like framing. Keep separation through spacing and hierarchy.

WORK GALLERY — PRESERVE OWNER'S INTERACTION DECISIONS
Show work immediately. Two columns desktop and one mobile. All industries visible in wrapping rows; no horizontal scrollbars. No search, visible result-count row, brand-name caption, Film badge, duration, project arrow or gallery-card navigation.
Media plus all assigned services in bottom-left labels and pause/play top-right. Preserve muted looping autoplay, offscreen pausing, poster fallback, manual pause and reduced-motion support. Services remain a secondary refinement in the Filters panel; preserve Apply/Cancel, URL-driven selection, pagination and enquiry.
Apply the quiet cream/content treatment of 04 to the gallery, retaining the emphatic typography shared by the reference set. Do not turn a work browsing screen into a full hero composition.

MOTION — INFERENCE TO TEST, NOT EVIDENCE FROM STATIC IMAGES
If metallic details animate, use slow controlled drift or restrained light movement at edges. Keep browsing controls responsive and modest; wrapping pills must not displace neighboring rows. Preserve reduced motion. The still references do not authorize elaborate scroll sequences or additional autoplay with sound.

CONTENT AND REVIEW
These images are style evidence, not proof of business claims. Do not copy generated misspellings, unverified statistics, scarcity offers, rating badges, client counts, logos or testimonials as factual site content.
The website copy and actual portfolio media come from the content inventory.
Before extending the system, compare a small rendered specimen covering one dark statement, one cream content block, typography, an action, utility pills and a work frame with these references. Evaluate type weight, clear space, red intensity, metallic detail placement, radii and mobile readability together.
Latest owner instructions override all prior documents. The approved-reference direction resolves the older false choice between cinematic branding and minimal browsing; page-specific treatments should share the same visual identity.
