# Brand profile direction

## The invitation

Hero: MAKE YOUR BRAND ROAR.

The brand you imagine deserves to exist.

Button: Show us your world.

The metal button opens into a glass website field. Enter advances to email or phone. Back preserves the website. A confirmed lead save is required before showing success.

## Give people a reason to create a profile

Keep account creation optional after the introduction. Offer a place to save their brand brief and return to the conversation. Do not make an account a condition of exploring the agency.

Suggested sequence:

1. Website, then preferred contact.
2. Optional details: name, brand name, role, what they are building, and what they want to change.
3. Offer “Save my brand profile” with a clear explanation of what will be saved.
4. Verify ownership of the email or phone before connecting any existing lead or workspace. Matching a typed contact is not proof of identity.
5. Open a simple profile: brand essentials, goals, reference links and assets, plus conversation history the person is entitled to access.
6. When a project begins, connect the verified profile to the existing client portal for projects, approvals and assets. Keep internal agency notes private.

For the first release, email verification is the simpler account route. Visitors who supplied a phone can still be contacted without an account; ask for an email only if they choose to save a profile.

## Current implementation boundary

The hero saves an introduction through the existing lead endpoint. It does not create a user account. The local preview has no Firebase Admin configuration, so it correctly reports that details were not saved and provides the public contact email. Production storage and account verification must be configured and tested before promising saved profiles.

## What changed

The opening has one fixed promise. The kinetic word artwork remains at the closing CTA. An inline invitation replaces the modal. The laurel statements replace the opening counts, with flags treated as a separate international detail. Shorter gaps connect the hero with the next chapter. The copy speaks about artistic direction and collaboration, with the services supplying concrete context.
