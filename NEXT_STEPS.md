## 2. `/services/web` — remaining polish (the page is good; make it *premium*)

The structure converts; these moves kill the last of the "flat" feeling.

### 2a. Dark "cinema" proof band  *(recommended next)*
**Proposal:** one full-bleed near-black section after the Spec-Sheet phone — the page's single tonal
break. A large outcome statement + 3–5 real client faces in a horizontal strip with one-line results.
**Why:** rhythm (white→black→white) = perceived production value; consensus proof ("people like me
hired them"); Von Restorff makes the next white section pop.

## 3. `/services/ai` — NEW flagship (highest impact)

Full concept in `SITE_REDESIGN_BRIEF.md` Part 4. The innovation story: voice agents + CRM +
automation as a *partner*, not chatbots. Signature beat: a phone call that **types itself out on
scroll** (agent answers, qualifies, books, CRM card slides in) → "Talk to one" opens Nova **live** —
the demo IS the product.

**Design intent:** black cinema ground (authority), the live-call scene as the proof no competitor can
fake, an industry rail (clinics / real estate / restaurants / dealerships / contractors / hotels) each
citing a real `en.ts` testimonial.

**Questions:**
- Q8. **Public name:** "AI & Smart Systems" with **MOD SYSTEMS** as the product line, or plain
  "Smart Systems & AI"? (Blocker — sets nav + routes + copy.)
- Q9. **Hero video** — your big AI/automation clip URL (goes in the curtain)?
- Q10. Real **voice-agent transcript** to script the live-call scene (or I draft a realistic one)?
- Q11. Prices for the two AI offers (project "System Build" + monthly "Autopilot")?
