# AI Systems Master Page Specification

## Product outcome

`/services/ai` must help an owner understand the commercial value of a connected AI operating system, identify the first system worth building, and start a consultation without needing to understand AI tooling.

## Experience principles

1. **One living system.** One WebGL canvas and one particle population tell the full story. Never stack independent effects.
2. **Crown, not lion.** The opening and closing brand form is a sparse geometric crown that remains recognizable at the 77-particle mobile budget.
3. **Motion explains.** Crown → release → immersive field → connected constellation → energy spine → operating system → crown. Decorative motion cannot compete with copy.
4. **Glass has a job.** Liquid glass appears only on navigation, the four-system selector, the delivery roadmap, ROI proof, and final decision panel.
5. **Outcome-first selling.** Copy leads with time, response, revenue opportunity, and operational ease. It avoids tool catalogues and unsupported promises.
6. **A calm premium rhythm.** Large type, generous space, alternating editorial layouts, persistent orientation, and one primary action.

## Commercial journey

1. Outcome promise — the business keeps moving when the owner steps away.
2. Cost of fragmentation — delayed responses, lost follow-up, repeated work.
3. Connected intelligence — signals become context and useful next actions.
4. Four high-value systems — Capture & Convert; Serve & Retain; Run & Fulfill; See & Scale.
5. Operating-system flow — every function improves the next.
6. Delivery mechanism — Blueprint, Build, Optimize.
7. ROI lens — calculate recoverable capacity without presenting projections as guarantees.
8. Ongoing partnership — launch focused, keep the system maintained and improving.
9. Decision — reformed crown and a clear consultation CTA.

## Responsive and performance contract

| Tier | Particle budget | DPR cap | Post-processing | Ambient detail |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Mobile / low capability | 77 | 1.25 | None | No dust, trails, plexus, or pointer math |
| Tablet | 177 | 1.45 | None | Short trails, restrained dust |
| Desktop | 777 | 1.65 | Subtle bloom | Trails, dust, plexus |
| High-end wide desktop | 1,377 | 1.8 | Subtle bloom | Full restrained detail |

- Crown points are mathematically generated; no GLB fetch, decode, or surface sampling.
- Mobile point sprites use a crisp opaque core and controlled halo, never a faded disc.
- Mobile glass uses translucent gradients and edge lighting, not `backdrop-filter`.
- The render loop pauses when the tab is hidden and lowers cadence while visually idle.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` renders a composed static state.
- Copy never uses blur-based transitions.

## Interaction contract

- Floating section navigation uses native anchors and `aria-current`.
- Scroll assistance is proximity-only; touch scrolling is never captured.
- Tab controls are keyboard operable and retain a visible focus state.
- CTAs open the existing Nova consultation experience.
- Small interactions use position, opacity, edge light, and color. No bounce, continuous card shimmer, or novelty cursor.

## Acceptance criteria

- No lion-head language or lion model request remains in the AI page implementation.
- Exactly one fixed WebGL canvas is present.
- Crown silhouette is legible at 390px viewport width and 77 particles.
- Main body copy is at least 18px on desktop and 17px on mobile; labels are at least 13px.
- The four-system selector, delivery roadmap, ROI panel, and final CTA use the shared glass surface.
- Mobile navigation and CTA do not obscure focused controls or final-page content.
- TypeScript, lint, and production build pass, or any repository-wide pre-existing blocker is documented with exact output.
- Browser verification checks load, console errors, navigation, tabs, ROI controls, CTA, 390px layout, and reduced motion.
