# Seasonal Work direction — discussion draft

7 October 2026. The owner wants stronger examples with Black Friday and Christmas relevance. Clarification is pending on whether the disliked examples are gallery work, client result stories, or both, and whether to use existing seasonal work, labeled concepts, or a mix.

## Recommended structure

Keep industry as the main filter. Treat Black Friday and Christmas as campaign occasions, not industries. Add a compact seasonal selection within the complete Work page after choosing appropriate assets; do not add a separate destination or force more filters.

Show a cohesive campaign per example: offer/creative direction, landing-page or shop application, and a few social/email applications. Retain the media-first framing and put purpose/proof alongside the relevant selection rather than adding captions to every video.

Possible briefs, not claims of completed work:

- Retail/e-commerce: Black Friday offer, collection landing page and campaign assets; Christmas gift selection, packaging and email sequence.
- Hospitality: seasonal stays or dining packages, gift vouchers and a clear booking path.
- Wellness: giftable experiences, vouchers and campaign imagery linked to a simple booking flow.

The current work and testimonial data contains no explicit Black Friday or Christmas classification. Do not relabel it as seasonal work without confirming the actual project context. Clearly identify newly created concepts; concepts have intended applications, not historical client results.

## More relevant proof

Prefer two or three specific client accounts over an expanded wall of general praise. Useful details: the campaign objective, what was delivered, timeframe, client-reported change and permission to publish the attribution. Preserve exact quotes and any qualifiers. Never imply a testimonial concerns a holiday campaign when it does not.

## Headline

Current local working direction:

YOUR VISION. YOUR IMPACT.
Their trust. Their loyalty.

French equivalent:

VOTRE VISION. VOTRE IMPACT.
Leur confiance. Leur fidélité.

The owner's alternatives include “Your story” and “Their experience.” Recommendation: do not combine all six short statements in the opening. Four keeps the brand-to-customer relationship clear and leaves room for the gallery. Story and experience can inform the campaign examples and client proof instead.
